What we store

An account email address, an optional public profile (username, display name, bio, avatar), privacy settings, purchase and license records, and a list of registered installations (app version and last-seen time).

What we never store

Manuscript contents, document text, raw keystrokes, clipboard contents, and private document names are never uploaded. The macOS app keeps your writing data on your Mac.

What we share

Payments are processed by Stripe. Only the aggregate statistics you explicitly choose to publish may appear on public profiles or leaderboards.

Your choices

You control profile visibility, leaderboard participation, and statistic visibility independently. You may delete your account; legally required transaction records are retained as described in the terms.
